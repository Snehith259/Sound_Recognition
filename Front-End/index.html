Neural Frameworks

Deep Learning Architectures for Sound Recognition

Complementary fusion of linear and nonlinear acoustic features as validated in recent EURASIP signal processing research.

Linear Features

SResNet (Squeezed ResNet)

A squeezed residual network designed to extract stationary and harmonic linear representations. Employs lightweight bottleneck residual blocks to capture fine-grained mel-frequency cepstral coefficients without overfitting.

Nonlinear Features

CNN-GRU Hybrid

Combines 2D convolutional layers with Gated Recurrent Units. The CNN frontend learns localized spectro-temporal patterns, while the recurrent GRU layers model long-term non-stationary dynamics and acoustic transients.

Methodology

Acoustic Signal Processing Workflow

Step-by-step pipeline from raw audio ingestion to multi-class posterior probability fusion.

01

Resampling & Active Segment

Audio is resampled to a standardized 22,050 Hz (5.0s duration, 110,250 samples), peak-normalized to [-1.0, 1.0], and scanned for the longest energetic acoustic activity window.

22.05 kHz • Peak Norm • VAD
→
02

Dual-Modal Feature Extraction

Extracts 128-band Log-Mel spectrograms & 40-MFCCs (macroscopic linear/harmonic) alongside 128-PAA Threshold-Free Recurrence Plots (microscopic chaotic dynamics).

128-Mel • 40-MFCC • 128x128 RP
→
03

Stratified 5-Fold Validation

Dataset is split into 5 official balanced folds with strictly disjoint recording sources to ensure zero data leakage between training, validation, and test partitions.

5 Folds • Zero Leakage • PyTorch
→
04

Dual-Modal Fusion & SwiGLU

Cross-attention merges continuous phase recurrence with spectral energy maps, processed through SwiGLU-gated SResNet to suppress ambient noise and produce >97.8% accuracy.

DMFF Cross-Attn • SwiGLU SResNet
