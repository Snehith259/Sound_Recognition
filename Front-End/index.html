Interpreting Model Confidence & OOD Safety

Understanding when to trust predictions and how anomalies are handled

🟢 High score A model probability is not a correctness guarantee. Calibration has not been verified yet.
🟡 50% – 84% A lower model probability can indicate ambiguity; review the ranked alternatives.
🔴 < 50% / OOD Low model probability or an out-of-scope rule; this result may need manual review.

Neural Architecture & Research Pipeline

Engineering methodology: raw audio ingestion, shared feature extraction, four classifier architectures, and evaluation reports on ESC-50.

Phase 01

End-to-End Audio Ingestion & Feature Pipeline

Deterministic 4-stage processing flow converting raw sound waves into multimodal neural tensors

01

Resampling & Active Segment

Resample to 22,050 Hz, normalize peaks, and isolate the highest-energy audio segment.

22.05 kHz • Peak Norm • VAD
→
02

Dual-Modal Feature Extraction

Extract 128-band Log-Mel spectrograms, 40 MFCCs, and 128×128 Recurrence Plots in parallel.

128-Mel • 40-MFCC • 128x128 RP
→
03

Stratified 5-Fold Validation

Split into 5 stratified folds with disjoint recording sources for leak-free evaluation.

5 Folds • Zero Leakage • PyTorch
→
04

Dual-Modal Fusion & SwiGLU

Fuse spectral and recurrence features via cross-attention and SwiGLU-gated residual blocks.

DMFF Cross-Attn • SwiGLU SResNet
