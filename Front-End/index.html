Linear Features

SResNet (Squeezed ResNet)

A squeezed residual network designed to extract stationary and harmonic linear representations. Employs lightweight bottleneck residual blocks to capture fine-grained mel-frequency cepstral coefficients without overfitting.

Methodology

Acoustic Signal Processing Workflow

Step-by-step pipeline from raw audio ingestion to multi-class posterior probability fusion.

01

Audio Resampling & Windowing

Input audio clips are normalized to a uniform sample rate (16 kHz / 44.1 kHz) and divided into short overlapping Hanning windows (frame size 1024, hop size 512).

Dual Spectral Feature Extraction

Linear features are calculated via MFCCs with discrete cosine transforms, while nonlinear representations are captured through 128-band log-mel filterbanks.

03

Parallel Deep Network Inference

SResNet processes spatial spectral matrices while CNN-GRU models recurrent temporal sequences, extracting rich complementary latent embeddings.

04

Decision Fusion & Confidence

Combines stationary spectral representations from SResNet with temporal sequence transitions from CNN-GRU at the decision layer. The multi-stream consensus resolves acoustic ambiguities across complex soundscapes to produce the final classification and confidence metric.
